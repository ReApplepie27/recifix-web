Chicken Adobo

A rich and savory Filipino favorite with tender chicken in a soy-vinegar sauce.

Prep 10 minCook 35 minServes 4Easy

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken thighs
  • 60 ml soy sauce
  • 60 ml vinegar
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 120 ml water
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil

FROM PREP TO PLATE

Steps

  1. Marinate. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, garlic, and peppercorns. Rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Sear. Heat the oil and brown the chicken on both sides.
  3. Simmer. Add the marinade, vinegar, bay leaves, onion, and water. Simmer for 25 minutes.
  4. Finish. Reduce the sauce until glossy, then serve hot with rice.
